About the PI

Haozhi Ma
Tenure-Track Associate Professor, School of Life Sciences, Nanjing University · National Youth Talent Program Awardee

Haozhi Ma is a tenure-track associate professor at the School of Life Sciences, Nanjing University, and a National Youth Talent Program awardee. His research focuses on the macroecology of plant functional traits and biomes. He has published 14 SCI papers, including first/corresponding-author papers in Nature Ecology & Evolution and Nature Plants, with work positively cited by papers in Nature and Science. He received the 2022 China National Award for Outstanding Self-Funded Students Abroad and is a core member of an SNSF Advanced Grant project.
