Admissions & openings

The lab recruits PhD students, MSc students, undergraduate researchers, and postdoctoral fellows year-round. We value curiosity, self-motivation, and teamwork — your background need not be limited to ecology.

Open positions

Who we are looking for

PhD students

We admit direct-PhD or combined MSc–PhD students each year, working on plant functional traits and biome macroecology, for those with strong interest in macroecology and global-change biology and a willingness to commit to data-intensive research.

MSc students

Students with backgrounds in ecology, biological science, geography, statistics, or computer science are welcome to apply. Admitted students can join projects on biome classification and plant trait distributions.

Postdoctoral researchers

We recruit postdocs year-round, supported by Nanjing University's postdoctoral programs and lab funding, offering competitive salary and independent research space. See the university's postdoctoral call; feel free to email us.

Undergraduate researchers

We offer research training (SCI), innovation programs, and thesis projects to Nanjing University undergraduates. No prior programming background is required, but a willingness to learn R or Python is expected.

How to join the lab

01
Introduce yourself by email
Send an email to haozhi.ma@nju.edu.cn with a brief introduction of your background, research interests, and motivation to join, attaching your CV and academic transcript.
02
A short conversation
Suitable applicants will be invited to an in-person or online chat, to see whether their interests align with the expectations of the lab.
03
Apply through official channels
Graduate students apply through NJU's official graduate admissions process; postdocs should refer to the university and school recruitment calls.
