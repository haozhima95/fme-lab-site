Collaboration welcome

The lab maintains long-term collaborations with the Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Snow and Landscape Research (WSL), ETH Zürich, and other institutions. We welcome collaboration in many forms — data sharing, joint publications, co-supervised students, and visiting scholars — and look forward to advancing macroecological research on biomes and plant functional traits together with colleagues in China and abroad.
